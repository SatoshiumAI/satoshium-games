Coalition Signals

Trust inference, shifting alignments, and the signals that shape coalition formation.

A multi-actor coordination environment exploring how alliances form, harden, fracture, or dissolve under pressure. This module extends the suite from bilateral strategic logic into systems where outcome structure is shaped by relationships, not just moves. Observe how small shifts in trust, signaling, and restraint can reorganize the entire strategic field.
